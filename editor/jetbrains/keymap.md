# Keymap

Settings > **Keymap**

---

## Lookup

Change selecting from suggestions.

1. Remap `Choose Lookup Item Replace`, `Select Next Completion Option` and `Select Previous Completion Option` &rarr; `Ctrl` + `Y`, `J` and `K`
2. Remove `Tab` from `Choose Lookup Item Replace` (to make tab to work normally even when suggestion is opened)

### Troubleshooting

If `Ctrl + K` for lookup is not working:

Settings > Editor > **Vim** > Find **Ctrl+K** from `Shortcuts` and change the `Handler` to **IDE**
